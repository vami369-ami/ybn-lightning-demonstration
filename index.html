How the demonstration works

1
Detection
The EFM at YBN is shown with an indicative 1 km detection zone.
2
Warning
The hooter system provides an indicative 2.5 km radius coverage, corresponding to the stated 5 km range.
3
Protection
Lightning arrester protection is represented by an indicative 110 m radius around each protection point.

Community reach
Schools, colleges, Panchayat, markets and religious places are shown to explain how warning coverage relates to community locations.
